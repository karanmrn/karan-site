## Working thesis

AI-native data systems still depend on familiar data engineering fundamentals:
clear contracts, reliable transformations, governed access, and observable
failure modes.

## Why this matters

The useful AI layer depends on the system beneath it. If metrics are
inconsistent, access rules are unclear, or lineage is invisible, an AI interface
mostly makes ambiguity faster.

My bias is to treat AI systems as a new surface area on top of familiar
engineering fundamentals: source data, contracts, transformations, semantic
definitions, evaluation, and feedback loops.

That is less dramatic than treating every workflow as an agent problem, but it
is usually more useful.
