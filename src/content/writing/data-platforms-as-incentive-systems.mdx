## The observation

Teams do not optimise for the strategy on the wall. They optimise for the data and workflows that are easy to reach. If the trusted path is hard and the spreadsheet workaround is easy, the spreadsheet wins, and the platform team learns about it three quarters later in a board meeting.

## Incentive design, not data modelling

The interesting question is not only "what is the right schema." It is "what does the platform make easy, and what does it make uncomfortable." Every governed model is also a nudge. Every undocumented dataset is also a permission slip.

## What "elegant simplicity" actually means

Elegant simplicity is not minimalism for its own sake. It is the recognition that every layer of complexity is a tax on the trusted path. The fewer steps between a question and a defensible answer, the more often the defensible answer wins.

## The platform a Sales team will actually use

The best data platform is not the one with the most complete architecture diagram. It is the one that changes day-to-day behaviour. A Sales team will use the system that helps them answer a question before the meeting starts, not the one that requires a separate explanation from the data team.

That is the standard I want my work to meet: trusted definitions, fast paths to answers, and enough governance that people stop rebuilding the same number in private.
